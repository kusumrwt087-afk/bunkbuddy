BunkBuddy is a free, offline-first attendance tracker made for students.
Your data stays on your device — no accounts, no tracking, no ads. Ever.

🎓 About BunkBuddy

BunkBuddy is the fastest way to answer one question: "Kitni classes aur bunk kar sakta hoon?"

Add your subjects once. From then on, just tap ✅ or ❌ after each class. BunkBuddy instantly tells you your attendance %, how many classes you can still safely miss, and how many you need to attend in a row to recover.

No login. No ads. No tracking.
Your data is saved only on your device using your browser's local storage. It never leaves your phone.

Built for students who hate doing attendance math. Works offline. Free forever.
